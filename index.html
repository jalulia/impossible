03 Color

Orange.
Blue. Depth.

Eight colors. Black creates the space; Solar Ember and Blue Sky carry the energy at full strength. Navy, Sky Pale and Ember Soft only connect them inside a field.

Make it real.
Create the
conditions.
Details matter.

Keep flat colors at full opacity. A field can interpolate between them; those transitions are not additional palette tokens. Black carries reading text. Blue Violet on White is 4.476:1: large type only. Blue Sky on White is 2.848:1: use that pair for marks and decorative fields.

Contrast values apply to the flat pairs shown.
Keep body text on a stable, high-contrast surface.

04 Field & motion

A field,
not a backdrop.

Broad color transitions, a dark interval, slow deformation, and fine grain. Move the field without moving the reader’s place.

complexity.

Slow deformationTwo field rates: +0.020 and −0.017. The pattern changes continuously; it does not spin as a flat image.
Separate responseCursor refraction is local. Type remains still and selectable. The field never blocks reading.
A still stateReduced motion freezes the field. Pause is always available. Nothing depends on animation to communicate.
